Duration R²

Whisper's mean-pooled vectors predict segment length almost perfectly. Whisper adds fixed positional embeddings, so averaging over the first T frames encodes T. Control for duration when probing Whisper layers.

PHQ-8 probes at the interview level

Segment vectors are averaged per interview, weighted by duration. A logistic regression is trained on train and scored on dev, then trained on train + dev and scored on test. Its regularization is picked by 5-fold CV inside the training interviews. The shaded band is a 95% bootstrap interval over test interviews.

Definitions and method notes
  • Segments are participant speech only. Ellie's lines are never in a segment's audio. Setup chatter before Ellie's first line is the only participant speech left out.
  • Fillers are um, uh, mm, mhm, hmm and similar tokens. Hedges are phrases such as "I don't know", "kind of", "maybe" and "I think". Both are counted per 100 words.
  • Anisotropy is the mean pairwise cosine similarity of segment vectors, where 0 means directions are spread evenly.
  • eGeMAPS features are z-scored before the geometry measures.
  • The code that made every table is in the Sources panel and in scripts/analyze.py in the speech-lab-26 repository.
